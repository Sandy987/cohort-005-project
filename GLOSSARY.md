# Cadence

An online course platform where Students buy Courses, alone or for a team, and can ask for their money back under the Refund Policy.

## People

**Student**:
A person who buys and takes Courses.
_Avoid_: Learner, user (a user is any account: Student, Instructor or Admin)

## Buying

**Purchase**:
One payment for a Course, made by a Student for themselves or for a team.

**Team Purchase**:
A Purchase of several Seats for a Course, made on behalf of a team. The buyer does not get access themselves.

**Seat**:
One place in a Team Purchase. It is either unclaimed or claimed by a Student.
_Avoid_: Coupon (the code that is handed out for claiming a Seat is not a discount)

**Claim**:
A Student taking an unclaimed Seat for themselves, which gives them an Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Redeem

**Enrollment**:
A Student's access to a Course, which comes either from their own Purchase or from a claimed Seat. A Refund ends the Enrollment.

**List Price**:
The Course's current price, before any PPP adjustment.
_Avoid_: Original price

**PPP Price**:
The List Price reduced for the buyer's country, fixed at the time of the Purchase.
_Avoid_: Your price, discount (on its own)

## Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Student's request to have a Purchase refunded. It is Requested, then Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back when a Refund Request is Approved. A Declined request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, cancellation

**Refund Policy**:
The rules that decide whether a Purchase is eligible for a Refund.

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase, counting both the day of purchase and the last day, during which a Refund Request may be made.

**Refund Amount**:
The amount actually paid for the Purchase being refunded, never the Course's current list price. For a Team Purchase, this is the full amount paid for all Seats.

**Chargeback**:
A dispute that a cardholder raises with their bank, outside Cadence. It is not part of the Refund flow and must never be used as a name for a Refund Request.
